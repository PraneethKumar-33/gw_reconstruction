[Shape 4]:
Recovering weak signals from changing detector noise
---
[Shape 6]:
The problem
---
[Shape 7]:
Reconstruct the time-domain waveform while suppressing noise, drift and short instrumental glitches.
---
[Shape 9]:
Why it matters
---
[Shape 10]:
Reliable waveform recovery can support rapid follow-up and the study of compact-object mergers.
---
[Shape 12]:
Existing limitation
---
[Shape 13]:
Template-based searches depend on waveform coverage. Detection accuracy alone does not measure reconstruction fidelity.
---
[Shape 15]:
Project question
---
[Shape 16]:
How do local convolutions, global attention and an encoder-decoder respond to the same difficult conditions?
---
[Shape 17]:
Scope: waveform reconstruction and denoising. Detection significance is not evaluated.

=== SLIDE 7 ===
[Shape 2]:
Data preprocessing and leakage assessment
---
[Shape 3]:
07 / 13
---
[Shape 5]:
Valid intervals
---
[Shape 6]:
Exclude NaNs; retain contiguous chunks of at least 16 seconds.
---
[Shape 8]:
Filter and whiten
---
[Shape 9]:
Bandpass 20–500 Hz, then whitening with 4 s FFT / 2 s overlap.
---
[Shape 11]:
Segment and inject
---
[Shape 12]:
Trim 2 s edges; form 4,096-point windows with 50% overlap.
Scale injected waveforms by L2 norm and randomize time shifts.

Limitations, future scope and conclusion

[Shape 5]:
Current limitations
---
[Shape 6]:
Shared noise pool across splits; one detector and 1-second windows.
Low-mass tests use a non-tidal waveform approximation.
Real-event recovery and deployment latency need stronger validation.
---
[Shape 8]:
Future scope
---
[Shape 9]:
Split by GPS interval before preprocessing.
Add H1 + L1, longer contexts and tidal BNS waveforms.
Repeat seeds, quantify uncertainty and test independent events.

[Shape 10]:
Conclusion
---
[Shape 11]:
The project implements a working comparative reconstruction pipeline.
TCN leads on glitches, Transformer on faint signals, and the autoencoder
on stationary data in the recorded experiments.
---
[Shape 12]:
Model choice depends on the signal and noise condition.
---
[Shape 13]:
Evidence supports comparative results within this dataset, not a universal or deployment-ready detector.
